Compass Rewards — Program Guide for Team Members

Contoso Home & Auto · Internal training reference · Version 3.2 (effective February 1, 2026)

Contoso Home & Auto and Compass Rewards are fictitious. This guide was written for Microsoft 365 Copilot training. Any resemblance to a real company, program or person is coincidental.

1. What Compass Rewards is

Compass Rewards is the free loyalty program for Contoso Home & Auto and its partner banners. Members earn Compass Cash, an in-program currency where 1 Compass Cash = $1 toward eligible purchases.

Members can collect with:

  • the Compass Rewards app (digital card)
  • a physical Compass Rewards card
  • the Compass Rewards credit card (issued by a partner bank)
  • their phone number at the till

Bonus events (for example "Double Cash" or "3x Cash") multiply the base earn rate only. Bonus Compass Cash posts within 10 business days after the event ends.

3. Membership tiers

Tiers are based on eligible spend in the previous 12 months, reviewed on the first day of each month.

TierQualifying spend (rolling 12 months)Earn multiplierKey perks
Explorer$0+1.0xMember pricing, app offers, birthday bonus
Trailblazer$1,000+1.25xEverything in Explorer, plus free standard tire installation once per season
Summit$3,000+1.5xEverything in Trailblazer, plus early-access sales, free seasonal tire storage, priority service booking

Members who drop below a threshold move down one tier at the next monthly review. There is no grace period in version 3.2.

4. Redeeming Compass Cash

  • Minimum redemption is $10 Compass Cash, then in any amount.
  • Compass Cash can be combined with any payment type at the till, online or in the app.
  • Compass Cash cannot be redeemed for gift cards, the credit card bill, partner fuel or taxes on services where local rules prevent it.
  • Returns: Compass Cash used on a returned item is refunded as Compass Cash.

5. Expiry and inactivity

  • Compass Cash does not expire while the account is active.
  • An account is inactive after 18 months with no earn or redeem activity (was 24 months before February 1, 2026).
  • Members receive an email or app notice 60 days and 14 days before an inactive balance expires, if they have opted in to communications.

6. Missing Compass Cash

  1. Ask the member for the receipt or order number.
  2. In-store purchases: team members can add missing Compass Cash up to 30 days after purchase in the point-of-sale "Member adjustments" screen.
  3. Online orders and Auto Service work orders: the member submits the receipt in the app (Help > Missing Compass Cash). Most adjustments post within 48 hours.
  4. Bonus event cash: allow 10 business days after the event before submitting a request.
  5. Over $100 or older than 30 days: escalate to the Member Care contact centre.

7. What changed on February 1, 2026 (version 3.2)

  1. Partner fuel earn rate changed from 2.0% to 1.5%.
  2. Inactivity period shortened from 24 to 18 months.
  3. Tier grace period removed (members move down at the next monthly review).
  4. App-only offers expanded to all three tiers.

8. Talking points for team members

  • Lead with value: "Would you like to collect Compass Cash on today's purchase? It takes 30 seconds."
  • Use the app: show members how to check their balance, tier progress and personalized offers.
  • Be honest about changes: acknowledge the fuel earn change and point members to the FAQ in the app.
  • Never ask a member for their full credit card number to look up an account. Use phone number or card barcode.

9. Privacy reminders

  • Look up member accounts only to help the member in front of you.
  • Do not write member names, phone numbers or balances on paper or in personal notes.
  • Member data stays in approved systems. Do not paste member personal information into unapproved tools.

10. Quick FAQ

Can two people share an account? Yes. A household account can have up to 4 linked cards; all activity keeps the account active.

Does Compass Cash have a cash value? No. It can only be redeemed for eligible purchases.

Can a member transfer Compass Cash? Only within a household account.

What if the system is down at the till? Complete the sale, give the member the receipt and ask them to submit it in the app within 30 days.
