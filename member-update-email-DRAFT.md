**Subject:** Important Information Regarding Changes To The Compass Rewards Loyalty Program Terms And Conditions Effective Immediately

Dear Valued Compass Rewards Member,

We are writing to you today in order to inform you that, as part of our ongoing commitment to continuously enhancing and optimizing the member experience and ensuring the long-term sustainability of the program for all stakeholders, certain modifications have been made to the Compass Rewards Program Terms and Conditions which have been in effect as of February 1st 2026.

It should be noted that the earn rate at partner fuel locations has been adjusted from 2% to 1.5%, inactivity period has been reduced to 18 month (previously 24 months), and the tier grace period is no longer applicable. In addition app only offers are now available to all tiers which we think is very exciting!!!

Members who have not earned or redeemed for 18 months will have their Compass Cash balance expired, notifications may be sent if communications preferences permit. Members are encouraged to review the updated Terms and Conditions which are available on our website, the app, and in store upon request at the customer service desk during regular business hours.

We apologize for any inconvenience this may cause and we thank you for your continued loyalty. Should you have any questions or concerns please do not hesitate to reach out to our Member Care team who would be more then happy to assist you.
